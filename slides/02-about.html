About IDENA

A room for the woman beneath the noise

What is IDENA

The second edition of the Becoming Her Webinar — an annual virtual gathering hosted by young women in engineering.

Why IDENA

Identity is the first point of success. The way we see ourselves shapes every choice, every room, every becoming that follows.

Who is it for

Every young woman — whether figuring herself out or quietly rebuilding. By young women, for young women.
